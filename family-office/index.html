    Bring in a spreadsheet

    From Excel or Numbers, save the sheet as CSV, then choose it here. One row per holding. The first row names the columns; only kind and owner are needed, with symbol and qty for shares, gold and crypto, or value and currency for everything else. New owners are added by name. Nothing is changed until you press "Add these".

    Zakat, a rough estimate

    2.5% of what each person holds that counts for zakat, when it is above the nisab (the value of 85 grams of gold) and has been held for a full lunar year. Each person is worked out on their own. Scholars differ on shares, property and debts, so every holding below can be changed. A guide to take to your scholar or to Bait Al-Zakat, not a ruling.

    What counts

    The usual starting point for each kind: cash, gold, crypto, sukuk and shares in full; a home or a rented property not counted (its rent is counted once it sits in the bank). Change any holding to match your scholar's view.
