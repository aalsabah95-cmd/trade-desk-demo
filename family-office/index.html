Bring in a spreadsheet

From Excel or Numbers, save the sheet as CSV, then choose it here. One row per holding. The first row names the columns; only kind and owner are needed, with symbol and qty for shares, gold and crypto, or value and currency for everything else. New owners are added by name. Nothing is changed until you press "Add these".

Who owns what

The people and companies in the family. Each holding belongs to one of them; the Overview adds them up.

Settings

The same switch is on the Trade Desk. For US shares the Trade Desk's screen is used; for everything else you mark it yourself on each holding (an Islamic bank account, sukuk and gold usually pass; interest-bearing deposits and bonds do not). A screen, not a ruling.
