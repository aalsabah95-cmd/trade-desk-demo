Risk

Your fund

Your rules run together as one fund, the way a quant fund runs many strategies at once: when one is losing, another may be winning. Each strategy gets an equal share. Past prices only; not a forecast, not advice, and nothing is traded.

Test a rule

Run a rule on past prices and see how it would have done after costs, against simply holding: the return, the worst drop, the longest losing run. Then the tough test: the same rule judged on the most recent 30% of the years, and again with its number nudged, so a rule that only fits the past shows up. Past prices only; not a forecast, not advice, and nothing is traded.

The AI's rule ideas

AI team

Say "team NVDA" (or any stock you name) to the assistant. Nine checkers read the results and filings, the news, the mood online, the chart, insiders and Congress, big money, analysts, short sellers and the big picture; a bull and a bear argue; a trader sizes it by your rules; a risk team pokes holes in it, and its risk manager can say no; and your own rules judge it. If it passes, the team makes a pretend buy, scored after 30 and 90 days, so you can see how its arguments held up. It never picks a stock and never trades. Not advice.

How the team works

The pretend portfolio

The team trades $100,000 of pretend money: every call that passes your rules is a pretend buy, sold when its stop is hit or after 90 days. Each trade is sent to your phone with why. Measured against the S&P 500.
