Check a buy

Thinking of buying something? Put it in here. The desk sizes it to your limits, shows how often a trade like it worked before, checks whether it doubles up on something you own, and shows the latest news.

Possibilities

If you bought a stock and held it for a week, a month, a year: how often was it higher, and by how much? Counted from every day of its last ten years.
